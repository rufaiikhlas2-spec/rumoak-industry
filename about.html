Who We Are

A company serving both construction and agriculture.

RUMOAK INDUSTRY NIG LTD is a Nigerian company involved in construction and agricultural activities.

The company helps clients with the construction of houses, farmsteads, poultry facilities and fish ponds. It also deals in fish and other consumable farm animals.


Products can be supplied either live or processed, depending on the customer's needs. Bulk supply is also available for suitable products.
